Knight Bite is a cloud kitchen which delivers food to the nocturnals, giving equal emphasis on food, technology and logistics.

Knight Bite started with the idea to serve all the hungry nocturnals who found it difficult to order late-night munchies. Knight Bite brought together late-night eaters and helped them order delicious meals conveniently through the Knight Bite App and phone calls.

Knight Bite is one such concept which successfully adapted to the emerging market.


Customers loved the Knight Bite app because it made ordering much easier. Within two weeks of launch, the app was trending among food apps and was downloaded by more than 5000 users across Mangalore and Manipal.

Going forward, our aim is to bring smiles to many more faces and expand to cities across India and around the world.
