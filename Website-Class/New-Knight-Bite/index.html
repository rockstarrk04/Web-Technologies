Our story

Hot food that
actually arrives hot.

Knight Bite started with one stubborn idea: most delivered burgers turn up a disappointment — steamed bun, cold fries, sauce everywhere. That is fixable.

So we built the kitchen around fixing it. Everything cooked to order, portioned the same way every time, packed to survive the ride. Within two weeks of launch our app was trending in the food category across cities.
